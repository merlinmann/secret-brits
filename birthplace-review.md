### Place distinctions

- **Rupert Friend:** the fan-site reproduction of his 21 October 2017 Times interview gives Cambridge as his birthplace and describes his family's later move to Oxfordshire. The Television Academy gives Oxfordshire. Cambridge is used with the conflict noted. The original Times page was unavailable during verification. [Reproduced interview](https://rupertfriendfandotcom.wordpress.com/2017/10/21/the-times-interview-with-rupert-friend/), [original Times URL](https://www.thetimes.com/article/just-sitting-near-the-actor-rupert-friend-i-can-feel-my-sperm-count-rising-r5tnkxhxl), [Television Academy](https://www.televisionacademy.com/bios/rupert-friend).
- **Antony Starr:** the Television Academy gives Wellington; TV Insider’s heading gives Auckland and its biography gives Wellington. Rotten Tomatoes repeats that contradictory record. [Television Academy](https://www.televisionacademy.com/bios/antony-starr), [TV Insider](https://www.tvinsider.com/people/antony-starr/), [Rotten Tomatoes](https://static.rottentomatoes.com/celebrity/antony_starr).
- **Rufus Sewell:** the Television Academy, Broadway League and Guardian interview give Twickenham. Wikipedia gives Hammersmith without a clear birthplace citation. [Television Academy](https://www.televisionacademy.com/bios/rufus-sewell), [IBDB](https://www.ibdb.com/broadway-cast-staff/rufus-sewell-59608), [Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/film/2006/dec/08/4), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rufus_Sewell).
- **Blake Ritson:** TV Insider gives London as his birthplace. The Official London Theatre interview describes schooling in Reading, and Square Mile describes residence in London and New York. [TV Insider](https://www.tvinsider.com/people/blake-ritson/), [Official London Theatre](https://officiallondontheatre.com/news/blake-ritson-108853/), [Square Mile](https://squaremile.com/property/blake-ritson-interview/).
- **Bill Camp:** IBDB gives Massachusetts as his birthplace; the Boston Globe describes Groton as his childhood home. [IBDB](https://www.ibdb.com/broadway-cast-staff/bill-camp-70319), [Boston Globe](https://www.bostonglobe.com/arts/theater-art/2016/06/02/bill-camp-second-act/NIyfskTjXa7NZOrwLoKaTL/story.html).
